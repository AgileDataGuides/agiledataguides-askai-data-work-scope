# Mission Command Statement: AI Analyst

> The WHY for the AI Analyst work. An AgileData **Mission Command Statement**: the higher intent, the squad's intent, what success looks like, the boundaries, and the key tasks.
> The team's Mission Command Statement, transcribed to markdown.

**Mission Name:** AI Analyst

## Higher Intent

Put a trusted analyst in every person's hands, so anyone can ask questions of governed data in plain language and get fast, reliable, explainable answers, and decisions get made on data at the speed the business moves.

## Squad Intent

Build an AI Analyst that lets Information Consumers and Data Analysts consume data through plain-language conversation, grounded in our governed data and proven patterns. Start narrow in one well-understood domain with a human in the loop, then earn the trust to widen coverage. Work with the business domain teams so the AI Analyst fits existing workflows, we move forward together, not in silos.

## Success Looks Like

- Users get accurate, explainable answers in seconds, not days.
- 70%+ of common, repeatable questions answered without a human in the loop.
- 90% of the responses are accurate.
- Every answer traces back to governed data, with its definition shown.
- Information Consumers trust the answers enough to act on them.
- Data Analysts spend more time on new questions, less on repeat requests.

## Boundaries

- Answers grounded only in governed, trusted data, no made-up numbers.
- Every answer must be explainable and traceable to source and definition.
- A human approves any model, rule or definition change, no silent autonomous edits.
- Respect existing permissions, never expose data a user can't already see.
- No PII or sensitive data available to unapproved Information Consumers.
- When unsure, say so and hand off to the Data Team, don't guess.
- Stay within the agreed model-cost budget.

## Key Tasks

- Pick the first domain and the questions the AI Analyst must answer.
- Build the semantic layer (facts, measures and metrics, definitions, relationships) it will reason using.
- Implement plain-language question to validated query to explainable answer.
- Add guardrails: permission checks, source citations, graceful "I don't know".
- Set up human-in-the-loop review for proposed model and rule changes.
- Define eval metrics (accuracy, trust, adoption, cost) and a feedback loop.
- Pilot with a small group, gather feedback and learnings, then iterate.
