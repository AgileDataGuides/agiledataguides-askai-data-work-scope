**Product:** The AI Analyst

## Introduction

Today the AgileData team is excited to announce the AI Analyst now built into the AgileData Information Platform. We designed the AI Analyst with the working-backwards approach in mind: we started with you, the Information Consumer, and worked backwards to the one capability that would help you most. The AI Analyst lets you ask questions of your governed data in plain language and get fast, trustworthy, explainable answers. No code. No ticket. No waiting on the data team.

## The Problem

Information Consumers wait days for answers. To check a number or understand a trend, they raise a request, join the queue in front of the data team, and often get an answer that only raises more questions. Self-service BI tools assume they can write queries or know where the trusted data lives, so they fall back on stale spreadsheets, gut feel, or going without. Decisions slow down, or get made on shaky data.

## The Solution

The AI Analyst is built into the AgileData Information Platform. The Information Consumer asks a question in plain language; the AI Analyst turns it into a governed query and returns a fast, explainable answer, grounded in trusted data, with the definition and source shown. It respects existing permissions, explains anomalies and errors in plain English, and keeps a human in the loop for any change to a model, rule or definition. The data team still owns the truth; the consumer just stops waiting for it.
